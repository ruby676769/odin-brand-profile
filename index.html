Logo

The Eye.

A heavy uppercase ODIN wordmark. The red dot inside the O is Odin’s eye, set slightly forward so the mark looks ahead. On paper the eye is Odin Red; on a red background the eye turns paper white. The eye alone is the icon.

Brand aesthetic

Bold, minimal, high contrast.

Heavy, tight headlines set large. Flat full-bleed backgrounds in Odin Red, black or off-white. Square corners and hard dividing rules on a strict grid. Lots of space. Real photography of people and work: high contrast, natural light, often black and white with red as the only colour.

Always

  • Square corners
  • Full red, black or off-white backgrounds
  • One headline, one idea, ending in a red full stop
  • Real numbers with units
  • Real, high-contrast photography

Never

  • Gradients, glass, blur, glows or drop shadows
  • Navy, cream or purple
  • Rounded pill buttons or rounded cards
  • Script or handwritten fonts
  • Emoji, stock handshake photos, 3D blobs
Brand tone of voice

Confident, direct, specific.

We write like a senior partner talking to a CEO: short declarative sentences, plain English, real numbers. We let results do the bragging. No hype words (game-changing, unlock, elevate, supercharge), no emoji, no agency jargon, no hedging.

Say

“Cost per lead fell 32% in October.”
“We agree your KPIs before we start.”

Don’t say

“We supercharge your growth.”
“We pride ourselves on a bespoke approach.”

Model preferences

Strong natural or directional light, high contrast, often black and white with a single red element.
